Cornell Sports Analytics

We conduct independent research in small groups to learn more about various sports through the lens of data science.

See Our Projects Get In Touch

Who We Are:

Cornell Sports Analytics is the premier club for sports analytics research at Cornell. We strive to form an inclusive community of aspiring sports analysts from all backgrounds to better our understanding of sports. We encourage everyone to apply, regardless of background!

What We Do

  • Research and Analysis

    We split into small teams to work on passion projects involving the sports that members are interested in. We have access to almost any data source, fully funded by the club, so it's a great chance to investigate things you wouldn't normally be able to individually.

  • Present

    Each semester, we get together to present our work with food and drinks. It's a great chance to share what you've accomplished along with hearing about other happenings in various sports.

  • Learn Together

    Members trade skills in statistics, programming, and knowledge of the game. We form groups based on complementing skillsets and have a tight-knit community within the whole club.
