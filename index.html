Cornell Sports Analytics

We conduct independent research in small groups to learn more about various sports through the lens of data science.

See Our Projects Get In Touch

Who We Are:

Cornell Sports Analytics is the premier club for sports analytics research at Cornell. We strive to form an inclusive community of aspiring sports analysts from all backgrounds to better our understanding of sports. We encourage everyone to apply, regardless of background!

What We Do

  • Analyze

    We pick a question, collect the data, and find out whether the popular answer holds up.

  • Publish

    Every project ends as a written report. All of them are kept in our archive, sorted by year.

  • Learn Together

    Members trade skills in statistics, programming, and knowing the game, from a first project to the last.
