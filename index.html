Who we are

Cornell Sports Analytics is a student group for people who like sports and like numbers. We pick questions we actually want answered and work on them in small teams.

Our members come from many different areas of study. What we share is the habit of asking what the data says before deciding who is right.

What we do

  • Analyze

    We pick a question, collect the data, and find out whether the popular answer holds up.

  • Publish

    Every project ends as a written report. All of them are kept in our archive, sorted by year.

  • Learn together

    Members trade skills in statistics, programming, and knowing the game, from a first project to the last.
